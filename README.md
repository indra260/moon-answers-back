## The entry

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and promises — and not one of you ever wished me a happy birthday. So no, he isn't texting back.

(133 characters — well inside the 240-character gate.)

## Why it works

The line speaks as the Moon itself, answering directly: it names the wishes, the howls and the promises in the opening breath, so the first judging criterion is met on the first read. Its voice is wry and ancient — four billion years of being wished upon without a single wish back — and tired in exactly the way the brief asks for. The joke lands as a comeback because the turn comes at the end: the grand cosmic complaint collapses into the smallest, most familiar human plea, and the Moon refuses it flatly. Nothing is explained; the refusal *is* the punchline, and no character is wasted.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry line, exactly and with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). The program checks the gate first — 1 to 240 characters, not only whitespace, a single line — and fails loudly if the line ever violates it.
